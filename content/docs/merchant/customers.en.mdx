Your customer database is your store's most valuable asset. With Risev, you don't just collect phone numbers — you actively identify lapsed customers and bring them back into your store with 1-click WhatsApp outreach.

## 1. Filter Inactive Customers (Custom Days) 🔍

Every industry operates on a different customer return cycle:
* 💈 **Barbershops / Salons:** Customers typically return every **14 to 21 days**.
* ☕ **Cafes / Bakeries:** Customers return every **7 to 14 days**.
* ❄️ **Aircond & Auto Servicing:** Customers only need maintenance every **90 days** (3 months).

### How to Filter Inactive Customers:
1. Open the **Customers (👥)** tab in the bottom bar.
2. Tap the **"Filter Inactive"** button.
3. Choose a quick preset (**7 Days, 14 Days, 30 Days, 60 Days, 90 Days**) or type a **Custom Number of Days** into the text input box.
4. Tap **"Filter Customers"**.
5. Risev displays all customers whose last visit exceeds that timeframe!

---

## 2. 1-Click WhatsApp Follow-Up 📲

Once your inactive customer list is visible:
1. Beside each customer's name, you will see a **Green WhatsApp Icon**.
2. Tap the icon to launch WhatsApp directly with a pre-filled personalized message:
   > *"Hi [Customer Name], we miss seeing you at our store! This week we have a special treat just for you. Show this message at the counter to claim it!"*
3. Customers feel personally remembered and are inspired to return.

---

## 3. Top Spenders Leaderboard 🏆

At the bottom of the screen, Risev showcases your **Top Spenders Leaderboard (Live Stats)**:
* Highlights your most loyal VIPs who visit most frequently and spend the highest amount.
* Reward them with surprise perks to lock in lifelong brand loyalty.
