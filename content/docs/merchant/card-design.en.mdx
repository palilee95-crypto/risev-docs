Every business has a unique brand identity. With Risev, you have full creative control over your digital stamp card's look and feel, ensuring it looks sleek and premium on your customers' screens.

## Accessing the Card Designer

1. Open your Risev Merchant Console.
2. Navigate to the **Rewards (🎁)** tab in the bottom bar.
3. Click the **Card Design** sub-tab.

---

## 4 Core Customization Settings 🎨

### 1. Required Stamps
Decide how many stamps customers must collect before unlocking their reward:
* **5 Stamps:** Highly recommended for new businesses or monthly services (e.g. hair salons, car servicing) so customers feel the reward is quickly achievable.
* **10 Stamps:** Ideal for frequent daily/weekly purchases like coffee shops, bakeries, or eateries.
* **15 Stamps:** Great for high-ticket purchases or tiered VIP programs.

### 2. Custom Stamp Icons (18 Handcrafted Icons)
Choose an icon tailored to your business:
* ☕ **Cafe & Beverage:** Coffee Cup, Beer Mug, Cold Drinks.
* 🍔 **Food & Dining:** Pizza, Cake, Restaurant Plate, Ice Cream.
* ✂️ **Barbershop & Salons:** Hair Scissors, VIP Stars, Sparkles.
* 🚗 **Car Wash & Auto:** Sports Car, Discount Tag.
* 🛍️ **Retail & Boutiques:** Shopping Bag, Gift Box, Card.
* 🏋️ **Fitness:** Gym Barbell.

### 3. Brand Colors & Typography
* **Card Background:** Select from curated luxury themes like *Carbon Black, Deep Indigo, Emerald Green, Wine Crimson, Amber Gold*, or input your custom brand HEX color.
* **Stamp Ink Color:** The color that fills up when a stamp is awarded (e.g. Amber Gold, Electric Blue, Emerald Green).
* **Text Color:** Contrasting white or dark text.

### 4. Reward Headline & Terms
* **Reward Headline:** State the reward clearly (e.g. *"FREE 1 Americano Coffee after 10 stamps"* or *"RM15 OFF Next Bill"*).
* **Expiry Days:** Stamp validity period (e.g. 30 or 90 days) to create a natural incentive to revisit.
