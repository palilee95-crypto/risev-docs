# Manual Stamping (Using Phone Number)

While over 90% of smartphones today support NFC, Risev guarantees **you will never miss a single customer**. If a customer has an older phone, low battery, or NFC turned off, your cashier can easily award stamps manually in just 5 seconds.

## How to Award Stamps Manually ⚡

1. Open your Risev Merchant app on your counter phone or tablet.
2. Tap the **Yellow Glowing Center Button** (QR Icon / Issue Stamps) in the bottom navigation bar or visit `/give`.
3. In the **Customer Phone Number** field, enter the customer's phone number (e.g. `0123456789`).
4. Select or enter the **Bill Amount**:
   * Quick preset buttons are available: **RM10, RM20, RM50, RM100**, or enter the exact receipt total.
5. Choose how many stamps to award (typically 1 stamp).
6. Tap the yellow **"AWARD STAMPS"** button.

---

## What Happens Next?

* The customer database updates immediately.
* The digital stamp is credited to their account.
* When the customer next visits your store link or taps the stand, their newly awarded stamp is already there!
