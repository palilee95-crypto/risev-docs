# Counter Operations

Tutorials for daily operations. Once you have finished *Start Here*, this is the
section you will come back to when something goes wrong at the counter.

## 5. Setting Up the Points System

Besides stamps, you can give customers **points** based on how much they spend.
Points can be exchanged for discounts.

<VideoTutorial
  step={5}
  title="Setting Up the Points System"
  subtitle="Turn ringgit into points, and points into discounts."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

Open **Rewards → Points Tiers**. You set:

- **How many ringgit = 1 point** — for example RM1 = 1 point.
- **How many points = RM1 discount** — for example 100 points = RM1.
- **Minimum redemption** — the lowest number of points before redemption is allowed.

## 6. How Customers Redeem Points & Vouchers

The redemption flow at the counter. Customers can show a QR code, or read out a
voucher code to the cashier.

<VideoTutorial
  step={6}
  title="How Customers Redeem Points & Vouchers"
  subtitle="The redemption flow at the counter — scan a QR or enter a code."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

Press the yellow button in the middle of the bottom menu to open the **Issue &
Redeem** screen. There are three tabs:

| Tab | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Scan Voucher QR** | The customer shows a QR code — scan it with the camera. |
| **Voucher Code** | The camera is not working — ask the customer to read out the `WV-XXXX-XXXX` code. |
| **Manual Issuance** | The customer has no phone or no internet. |

> **If the screen looks stuck**, press the Refresh (↻) icon in the top right
> corner to reload the page.

## 7. Setting Up Smart Google Review

Collect 5-star reviews automatically after a customer taps — no need to ask
manually.

<VideoTutorial
  step={7}
  title="Setting Up Smart Google Review"
  subtitle="Collect 5-star reviews automatically after a customer taps."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

Open **Profile → Google Reviews** and connect your Google Business location.
After a customer taps and the transaction completes, they will be asked whether
they want to leave a review.

> **Real reviews only.** The system does not create reviews. It only makes it
> easier for customers who already want to leave one.
