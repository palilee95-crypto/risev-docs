# Product & Service Catalogue

The catalogue is the list of what your shop sells. Without a catalogue, customers
cannot book online, and a "free item" reward cannot be claimed automatically.

## 8. Setting Up Your Product & Service Catalogue

Open **Bookings → Manage Services**, then press **Add Service**. Each item needs:

| Field | Example | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | `Chicken Nasi Kandar` | The name customers see. |
| **Price** | `12.50` | Use a decimal point, not a comma. |
| **Duration** | `30` minutes | How long this service takes. |
| **Category** | `Food` | To organise your menu. |

### Add-ons

If customers can choose extras — for example "Fried egg" +RM1.50 — set them under
**Options** inside the same item.

> **The add-on price is the EXTRA amount, not the full price.** If the item is
> RM12.50 and the egg adds RM1.50, enter `1.50` in the add-on field — not
> `14.00`. The system does the maths.

### Why the catalogue matters for rewards

If your reward is "a free drink", the reward needs to know **which drink**.
Without an item in the catalogue, the cashier has to type the reward value
manually every time — and that is easy to get wrong.

---

## Once the catalogue is done

With a complete catalogue you can:

- **Open online booking** — customers book themselves through your store link.
- **Give free-item rewards** — claimed automatically, with no manual typing.
- **See sales reports** — analytics by item, not just the overall total.

> **Review it after a week.** An item that never gets booked may have the wrong
> price, an unappealing photo, or an unclear name. Fix it early.
