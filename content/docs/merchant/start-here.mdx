# Start Here

If you have just received your Risev NFC Stand, start here. These four tutorials
take under 5 minutes in total, and after them your shop can start collecting
customer stamps.

> **Order matters.** Tutorial 1 connects the stand to your account. Without that
> step, the other tutorials will not work.

## 1. Setting Up Your Stand

Open the box, take out the activation card, and connect the stand to your store
account. There are two ways — tap your phone, or redeem the `STAND-XXXX-XXXX`
code manually.

<VideoTutorial
  step={1}
  title="Setting Up Your Stand"
  subtitle="Unbox the NFC stand and connect it to your store account."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

**What's in the box:**

1. **1x Risev Smart NFC Stand** — with a built-in NFC chip and a scratch-resistant QR code.
2. **1x Activation Card** — containing a unique `STAND-XXXX-XXXX` code.
3. **1x Quick Counter Guide**.

**Fastest way (10 seconds):** open your phone's browser, make sure you are logged
in to your Risev merchant account, then tap the back of your phone on the top of
the stand. The screen opens by itself and shows a yellow **"Pair Stand"** button.

**If your phone has no NFC:** go to **Profile → My Subscription**, enter the code
from the Activation Card in the **Stand Activation Code** field, and press
**"Redeem Stand"**.

Once it succeeds you get **500 customer slots for life**.

## 2. How Customers Tap & How You Approve

This is the flow that happens at the counter every day. The customer taps their
phone on the stand, and you press Approve on the dashboard.

<VideoTutorial
  step={2}
  title="How Customers Tap & How You Approve"
  subtitle="The customer taps their phone, you press Approve. The full counter flow."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

**On the customer's screen:** they tap their phone, the browser opens to *Claim
Your Stamps*, they enter their phone number, and press **"Claim Stamps Now"**.

**On your screen:** an **"NFC Stamp Claim!"** popup appears immediately. Enter
the bill amount and the number of stamps, then press **"Issue Stamps & Confirm"**.

> **Tip:** put the stand next to your payment terminal so the cashier can easily
> remind customers — *"Sir, you can tap your phone on this stand for free
> stamps."*

---

## 3. Custom Design for Your Brand

Change the card colours, icons and images so your stamp card looks like your
shop, not a generic template.

<VideoTutorial
  step={3}
  title="Custom Design for Your Brand"
  subtitle="Change the colours, icons and images so the card looks like your shop."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

Open **Rewards → Card Design**. You can change the card colour, stamp colour,
text colour, icon, and upload your own images for the front and back of the card.

> **Use your own images, not stock photos.** Customers recognise your shop when
> the card looks like your shop.

---

## 4. Setting Up Customer Rewards

Decide what customers get once they collect enough stamps — for example "10
stamps = 1 free drink".

<VideoTutorial
  step={4}
  title="Setting Up Customer Rewards"
  subtitle="Decide what customers get once they collect enough stamps."
  duration="~1 min"
/>

Open **Rewards**, and set how many stamps are needed to claim the reward. The
reward can be a free item, a discount, or an experience.

> **A reward must name an item.** If the reward is "a free drink", choose which
> drink from your catalogue. Without it, the cashier has to type the reward value
> manually at the counter every time.
