This is the heartbeat of your daily counter operations. Customers claim their stamps at the counter in seconds without needing to download any app from the App Store or Google Play.

## The 3-Step Counter Flow ⚡

### Step 1: Customer Taps the Stand
1. The customer taps their phone against the **Risev NFC Stand** on your counter (or scans the backup QR code).
2. Their mobile browser opens their branded digital stamp card immediately.
3. They enter their phone number (first time only) and tap **"Claim Stamps Now →"**.

### Step 2: Cashier Receives Live Alert
On your store tablet or cashier phone Dashboard:
1. An incoming stamp request appears instantly in the **Pending Requests** list with an audio chime.
2. The cashier sees the customer's name and mobile number.

### Step 3: Cashier Approves the Stamp
1. Enter the customer's **Bill Amount** (e.g. `25` for RM25).
2. Choose the number of stamps to give (e.g. `1`).
3. Tap the green **"Approve"** button.
4. The customer's phone vibrates with a satisfying physical ink stamp animation and their card is updated!
