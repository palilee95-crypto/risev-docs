# How to Register as a Risev Merchant

This guide walks you through every step to set up your merchant account — from phone number registration, automatic email verification, to your store management console.

**Time required:** Less than 2 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Enter Your Phone Number

Open [risev.app](https://risev.app) in your phone or PC browser:
1. Ensure the country code is set to **🇲🇾 +60** (Malaysia).
2. Enter your **mobile number** without the leading `0` (e.g. `123456789`).
3. Tap **"GET STARTED →"**.

> **New Number?** The system automatically detects new users and opens the quick registration form.  
> **Existing Number?** Simply type your password to log in directly.

---

## Step 2: Fill in Your Account Details

Fill in the basic profile fields:
* **Full Name:** Business owner's name.
* **Email Address:** Active email address (verification link will be sent here). Use Gmail, Outlook, or another provider — **iCloud Mail addresses are not supported** (`@icloud.com`, `@me.com`, `@mac.com`).
* **Password:** Secure password (at least 8 characters).
* **Confirm Password:** Re-enter matching password.

> **Do not use an iCloud email address.** Verification emails to iCloud are unreliable, so registration will reject `@icloud.com`, `@me.com`, and `@mac.com` addresses. Use Gmail, Outlook, or another provider instead.

Tap **"REGISTER & VERIFY 💛"**.

---

## Step 3: Magic Email Verification (Live Auto-Detection) ✉️

Right after registering, Risev sends a secure verification link to your email inbox:
1. Open your Email app (Gmail / Outlook / Apple Mail).
2. Look for the email from **Risev** titled *"Verify your Risev account"*.
3. Click the **"Confirm Email"** button.
4. **Magic Auto-Login:** Your Risev browser window will automatically detect the confirmation in real-time and **log in instantly** without needing to retype any passwords!

---

## Step 4: Complete Your Store Branding

Once inside your **Merchant Console**:
1. Set your **Store Name** and category (e.g. Cafe, Barbershop, Car Wash, Boutique).
2. Upload your brand logo to be displayed on customers' digital stamp cards.
3. Setup your welcome message and proceed to design your loyalty card.
