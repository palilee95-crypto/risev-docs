# FAQ & Troubleshooting

Answers to the most frequently asked questions by store owners, cashiers, and customers.

---

### Q: Why do some phones not detect the NFC stand?
**A:** 
1. **NFC Chip Placement:**
   * **iPhone:** The NFC antenna is at the very **top edge** near the back cameras. Ensure customers tap the top of their iPhone against the stand.
   * **Android (Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, etc.):** The NFC antenna is typically located in the **center back**. Ensure **NFC is toggled ON** in their phone's quick settings drawer.
2. **Thick Phone Cases or Magnetic Plates:** Heavy-duty cases or magnetic car-mount plates can dampen the radio signal. Ask the customer to tilt the top edge of their phone against the stand.
3. **Backup QR Code:** If their phone still doesn't react, ask them to scan the high-contrast **Backup QR Code** printed right beneath the NFC logo on the stand!
