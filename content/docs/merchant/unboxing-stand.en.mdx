## What's Inside the Box? 📦

When your TikTok Shop package arrives, verify you have:
1. **1x Risev Smart NFC Acrylic Stand** (with integrated NFC chip & scratch-resistant QR code).
2. **1x Activation Card** with your unique activation code (`STAND-XXXX-XXXX`).
3. **1x Quick Setup Counter Guide**.

---

## 2 Easy Ways to Pair Your Stand

Choose either method below to link your stand to your store account:

### 🟢 Method 1: Tap Your Phone on the Stand (Fastest - 10 Seconds)

![Quick Start Tutorial Guide](/uploads/90A0AF01-A582-453A-8F7E-25BBFB_1789359419552.png)

1. Open your mobile browser and log in to your Risev merchant account at [risev.app](https://risev.app).
2. **Tap the back of your phone** against the top section of the Risev Stand.
3. Your phone will immediately open the stand link.
4. The system will detect the unlinked stand and ask:
   > **"Unlinked Stand Detected — Link this stand to [Your Store Name]?"**
5. Tap the yellow **"Pair Stand"** button.
6. **Done!** Your stand is now permanently paired to your store with lifetime validity.

---

### 🟡 Method 2: Redeem Activation Code (STAND-XXXX-XXXX)





![ChatGPT_Image_Sep_14__2026__12](/uploads/ChatGPT_Image_Sep_14__2026__12_1789360144083.png)





If your setup phone does not have NFC:

1. Grab the **Activation Card** included inside the parcel.
2. Log in to your merchant console at [risev.app](https://risev.app).
3. Go to **Profile (👤) > My Subscription**.
4. In the **Stand Activation Code** section, type your unique code (e.g. `STAND-8821-4902`).
5. Click **"Redeem Stand"**.
6. Your **500 Customer Lifetime Capacity** (*No Expiry*) will be instantly unlocked!

## Best Spot on Your Counter 📍



![ChatGPT_Image_Sep_14__2026__12](/uploads/ChatGPT_Image_Sep_14__2026__12_1789360071051.png)


* **Right next to the Payment Terminal / DuitNow QR:** Place the stand directly where customers make their payments so your cashier can easily invite them:  
  > *"Feel free to tap your phone here to earn your free reward stamp!"*
* **Keep it Visible:** Avoid blocking the stand with receipt rolls or impulse products.
* **Test it Once:** Before opening to customers, tap your own phone on the stand to see your branded digital stamp card in action.
