# Counter SOP Cheatsheet (Printable)

> 💡 **Tip for Store Owners:** Print this page and tape it beside your POS register or counter. New cashiers will understand the system in under 60 seconds!

---

## 3 Core Counter Scenarios

### 🟢 SCENARIO 1: Customer Wants to Claim a Stamp (Standard NFC Tap)
1. Customer taps their phone against the **Risev Stand** on the counter.
2. Customer taps **"Claim Stamp"** on their mobile screen.
3. On your Dashboard screen, their name appears in **Pending Requests** with a notification chime.
4. Check the bill amount and tap the green **"APPROVE"** button.
5. *Done! The physical ink stamp animation stamps onto the customer's phone.*

---

### 🟡 SCENARIO 2: Customer Has No NFC (Use Phone Number)
1. Tap the **Yellow Center Button (QR)** in the bottom navigation.
2. Enter the customer's phone number (e.g. `0123456789`).
3. Enter the bill amount (e.g. RM30) and select 1 stamp.
4. Tap the yellow **"AWARD STAMPS"** button.
5. *Done! The stamp is instantly credited to their account.*

---

### 🔴 SCENARIO 3: Customer Wants to Redeem a Reward
1. Customer displays their reward QR code on their phone screen.
2. Cashier taps the **Yellow Center Button (QR)** > Tap **"Scan Voucher QR"**.
3. Point the camera at the customer's QR code.
4. Screen displays a green *Success* banner ➔ Hand the free reward to the customer!

---
