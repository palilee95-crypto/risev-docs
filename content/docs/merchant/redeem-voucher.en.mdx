# Scan & Redeem Reward Vouchers

When a customer successfully collects the target number of stamps (e.g. 10 out of 10 stamps), their digital loyalty card automatically activates a **Reward Voucher**. Here is the simple flow for your cashier to redeem it at the counter.

## The Counter Redemption Flow 🎁

```
[ Customer Displays Voucher QR on Phone ] ➔ [ Cashier Scans with Risev Camera ] ➔ [ Voucher Redeemed & Gift Awarded ]
```

### Step 1: Customer Unlocks the Voucher
1. The customer taps their phone on your stand or opens their card.
2. When the card reaches full stamps, a glowing **"Redeem Reward"** button appears.
3. The customer taps it, displaying a unique, time-sensitive voucher QR code.

### Step 2: Cashier Scans the QR Code
1. On your Risev counter device, tap the **Yellow Center Button** (`/give`).
2. Tap the card titled **"Scan Voucher QR Code"**.
3. The built-in Risev camera scanner will launch automatically.
4. Point the camera at the customer's QR code.

### Step 3: Instant Validation
* A green confirmation banner will appear with an audio chime:
  > **"Voucher Successfully Redeemed! - Reward: 1x Free Coffee"**
* The voucher is securely marked as redeemed to prevent reuse.
* The customer's loyalty card resets to begin their next stamp cycle!
