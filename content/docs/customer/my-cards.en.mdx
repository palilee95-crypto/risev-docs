# My Loyalty Cards

Once registered and logged in, you will be taken to your **Loyalty Cards Screen** — a sleek digital wallet holding all your loyalty stamp cards from different merchants.

---

## Understanding Your Digital Card

Each card displays key information:

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Store Logo** | Official merchant branding |
| **Store Name** | Merchant business name and category |
| **Stamp Counter** | Example: `3/10` means you have 3 stamps, 7 needed for the reward |
| **Stamp Icon** | Custom icon chosen by the store (coffee cup, star, gift, etc.) |
| **Reward Name** | The exciting reward you unlock once the card is complete |

---

## Card Interaction & Flip

Cards in your wallet are **stacked** realistically like cards in a physical wallet:

- **Tap** any card to select it — the card will slide up and expand.
- Tap to **flip** the card:
  - **Front Side**: Store name, animated stamp grid, progress counter
  - **Back Side**: Your personal QR code & membership details

---

## Your Personal Member QR Code

The **back of the card** features your unique **Personal QR Code** — this is your digital membership pass.

### How to use it:
1. Tap your card to select it.
2. **Flip the card** to reveal the QR code.
3. Show the QR code to the cashier.
4. The cashier scans it with their POS/Merchant app to award your stamps instantly without asking for your phone number.

---

## Real-Time Stamp Animations

When the cashier awards stamps:
- You will see a live **stamp pop animation** as stamps illuminate on your screen.
- Progress counter updates in real time (`3/10` → `4/10`).
- When full (`10/10`), celebration confetti triggers and your voucher is unlocked!
