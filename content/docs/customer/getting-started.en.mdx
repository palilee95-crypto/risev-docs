# Getting Started with Risev (Customer Guide)

Welcome! Risev is a digital loyalty program that allows you to **collect stamps and redeem rewards** at your favorite stores — **without downloading any mobile apps!**

---

## 🚀 How to Start Collecting Stamps at the Counter

There are **2 quick ways** to start with Risev:

### Method 1: Tap the NFC Stand (Fastest) ⚡
1. Ensure **NFC** is turned on in your smartphone:
   - **iPhone:** NFC is enabled by default (iPhone 7 and above).
   - **Android:** Settings → Connections → NFC → Toggle ON.
2. **Tap** the top/back of your phone against the **Risev NFC Stand** at the checkout counter.
3. Your mobile browser (Safari / Chrome) will open immediately displaying the store's digital card.

### Method 2: Scan QR Code
If your phone doesn't have NFC:
1. Open your phone's **Camera** app.
2. Scan the **QR Code** printed on the Risev stand.
3. Tap the link popup to open the loyalty card.

---

## 📝 Quick Frictionless Registration

When tapping a store's NFC Stand for the first time:
1. Enter your **Name** and **Phone Number**.
2. Tap **"Collect Stamp"**.
3. Done! Your first stamp is credited instantly to your card. No waiting for SMS OTPs at checkout!

---

## 🔒 Complete Account & Save Cards

To view all your cards from various merchants, track rewards, and redeem vouchers anytime:
1. Tap **"Complete Profile"** on your digital card.
2. Enter your email address and set a password.
3. Tap the verification link sent to your inbox.
4. You can now access your **Digital Card Wallet** from any device!

---

## ❓ Customer FAQs

**Q: Do I need to download an app from the App Store or Google Play?**  
A: No! Risev works 100% in your mobile browser with zero installation needed.

**Q: Can I collect stamps at different stores?**  
A: Yes! Each participating merchant has their own branded card, and all your cards are saved in your one Risev wallet.

**Q: How do I know when my card is full?**  
A: Your digital card will show your total stamps (e.g., 10/10) and the reward voucher will unlock automatically for redemption.
