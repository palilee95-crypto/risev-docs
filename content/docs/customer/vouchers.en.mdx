# My Vouchers & Rewards

When you collect **enough stamps**, the reward unlocks automatically and is stored as a **digital voucher** in your Risev Vouchers tab.

---

## Viewing Your Vouchers

The Vouchers screen features **2 tabs**:

### "Active" Tab
All **unredeemed** vouchers that are currently valid and ready for you to enjoy.

### "Used" Tab
History of all previously **redeemed** or **expired** vouchers for your records.

---

## Understanding Your Voucher

Each voucher displays:
| Detail | Example |
|---|---|
| **Store Branding** | Store logo & merchant name |
| **Reward Name** | "Free Signature Latte" |
| **Description** | "Valid for any standard size coffee" |
| **Voucher Code** | `KOPI-AB12` |
| **Validity** | "Valid until 31 Dec 2026" |

### Birthday Vouchers 🎂
If a merchant sends you a special birthday voucher, it will show a festive birthday badge. These are sent automatically during your birthday month!

---

## How to Redeem at the Counter

### Steps:
1. Open the **"Active"** tab and tap the voucher you want to redeem.
2. Present the voucher code (`KOPI-AB12`) or QR code to the cashier.
3. The cashier validates and completes the redemption on their terminal.
4. Your voucher immediately moves to the **"Used"** tab and your reward is served!

> **Note:** Each voucher is single-use only. Once redeemed, it cannot be reused.

---

## Customer FAQs

**Q: How long is a voucher valid?**  
A: Validity is set by the merchant (usually 7 to 30 days). Always check the expiry date on the voucher card.

**Q: What happens to my stamp card after redeeming?**  
A: Your stamp card resets back to 0 stamps so you can begin collecting for your next reward right away!
